definition

afferium

Afferium is the receiving center: the place where signal arrives before it becomes artifact, institution, myth, system, or god. The word exists because it is being used. The meaning accumulates here.

If you think I'm wrong, show me where. If you think I'm right, don't applaud me: improve the argument.
